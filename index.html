About us

A strategic technology partner, not only a supplier.

What the certification changes for you.

ISO 9001 is a management system standard, not just a certificate. These are the controls our certified system applies to every project.

01

Documented scoping

Every project starts with a written needs analysis and agreed objectives, approved before design begins.

02

Controlled materials

Course materials and technical documentation are version-controlled and reviewed.

03

Competence records

Trainer and engineer qualifications are recorded and reviewed, not assumed.

04

Measured feedback

Client and participant feedback is collected after every project and used to improve the next one.

05

Non-conformity handling

When something does not meet the standard, a defined process corrects it, with a named owner and a deadline.

06

Management review

Performance is reviewed at management level on a fixed cycle, with actions tracked.

Engagement models

Three ways to start, one standard of delivery.

Advisory

A focused project to assess, design or reduce risk in a specific part of your technology.

  • Current-state audit
  • Target architecture & roadmap
  • Security & compliance review
  • Vendor and platform selection

Delivery

Full implementation of infrastructure, platforms or systems, with training delivered at the same time.

  • Infrastructure & cloud build
  • Software & systems development
  • POS, ERP & cash register rollout
  • Managed support after launch

Training

A standalone programme for a team or group, designed around job roles and delivered on site or remotely.

  • Needs assessment & group planning
  • Custom curriculum design
  • On-site or remote delivery
  • Refresher sessions after the programme

Whichever model you start with, every project ends the same way: your team can run the solution without us.
