Who we are

A strategic technology partner, not just a vendor.

Founded in 2022 and headquartered in Dubai, Infinite Icon Technology is an IT consultancy built for the pace of the UAE and Africa markets. We work with banks, government entities, and fast growing businesses that need technology to move as fast as their ambitions.

Our approach is deliberately twofold: we design and implement the technology, and we train the people who will run it. That combination of advisory and capability building is what makes transformation stick after the project team leaves.

What the certification changes for you.

ISO 9001 is a system standard, not a training accolade. These are the specific controls a certified system puts around your programme.

Documented scoping

Every programme starts from a recorded needs analysis and an agreed set of learning objectives, signed off before design begins.

Controlled materials

Course materials are version-controlled and reviewed, so two cohorts six months apart receive equivalent content.

Trainer competence records

Instructor qualification and subject currency are recorded and reviewed rather than assumed.

Measured feedback

Delegate evaluation and outcome data are collected every cohort and fed into the next revision.

Non-conformity handling

When something falls short there is a defined corrective process with an owner and a closure date.

Management review

Programme performance is reviewed at management level on a fixed cycle, with actions tracked.

How we train

Training that survives contact with the job.

Most corporate training fails for the same reason: it teaches a system rather than a role, and it arrives after the work has already changed. Everything in our catalogue is built the other way round.

Role-based curricula

Content is mapped to job function, so each cohort learns what it will actually use rather than a tour of every menu.

Delivered alongside build

Training runs in parallel with implementation, not as an afterthought at handover when attention has moved on.

Your data, your process

Exercises use your configuration, your terminology and your real scenarios wherever policy allows.

Measured, not assumed

Diagnostics before delivery and assessment after, so you can see the change rather than take it on trust.

Refresher cycles

How we work

Four stages, run the same way every time.

Transformation only holds if the sequence is right: assess before you build, and train while you build — not after.

Stage 1

Assess

Audit current systems, skills and processes against your business objectives to find the most impactful starting point.

Stage 2

Design

Architect the technical solution and the accompanying training curriculum together, so capability is built in from day one.

Stage 3

Deliver

Implement infrastructure, software and platforms while running the training programme in parallel with your team.

Stage 4

Sustain

Managed support, refresher training and roadmap reviews keep the transformation compounding after launch.

Engagement models

Three ways to start, one standard of delivery.

Training

A standalone programme for a team or delegation, scoped to role and delivered on site or remotely.

  • Needs assessment & cohort mapping
  • Custom curriculum design
  • On-site or remote delivery
  • Post-programme refreshers

Advisory

A defined engagement to assess, architect or de-risk a specific part of your technology estate.

  • Current-state audit
  • Target architecture & roadmap
  • Security & compliance review
  • Vendor and platform selection

Delivery

Full implementation of infrastructure, platforms or systems, with training run in parallel.

  • Infrastructure & cloud build
  • Software & systems development
  • POS, ERP & cash register rollout
  • Managed support after launch

Every engagement — whichever model it starts as — ends the same way: your team able to run it without us.
